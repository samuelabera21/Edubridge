4.5 Student
The student is the central learner/user of EduBridge.
Their responsibility can be summarized as:
Learn → participate → complete activities → demonstrate learning → monitor progress → seek support → communicate when necessary
The student should not manage official school records. They interact with the educational information generated by teachers and the school, while performing their own learning activities through EduBridge.
The student's scope is primarily:
My profile → My classes → My learning → My attendance → My assessments → My assignments → My progress → My support → My communication

This is particularly important given the Ministry's education-sector emphasis on students being empowered and having their voices heard in school decision-making. ESDP VI explicitly discusses student participation and creating a supportive environment where students can actively participate in school decision-making. 
The student shall be able to:
	Submit school feedback.
	Give feedback about learning activities.
	Give feedback about learning resources.
	Report learning barriers.
	Participate in permitted school surveys.
	Submit suggestions.
	Provide feedback about support activities.
	Participate in student-centered school improvement activities where enabled.
This should be structured feedback, not an unrestricted social-media system.

4.5.23 Student AI Learning Assistant
This deserves its own capability.
The Student AI Assistant shall be able to help with:
Understanding
	Explain a difficult concept.
	Explain a topic step by step.
	Provide examples.
	Rephrase explanations.
	Explain mistakes.
	Answer learning questions.
Practice
	Generate practice questions.
	Generate quizzes.
	Provide hints.
	Adjust difficulty.
	Give immediate practice feedback.
	Create revision exercises.
Study planning
	Create a study plan.
	Organize upcoming assessments.
	Recommend revision priorities.
	Break large learning tasks into smaller tasks.
	Remind students about learning goals.
Personalized learning
	Use permitted learning-performance information to suggest areas for practice.
	Recommend appropriate learning resources.
	Suggest revision based on weaknesses.
	Suggest enrichment based on strengths.
Language/accessibility assistance
Where supported:
	Explain content in a simpler form.
	Support multilingual learning.
	Assist with reading comprehension.
	Provide alternative explanations.
	Support accessibility needs.
But there must be strong boundaries.
The AI should not:
	Complete an assessment dishonestly for the student.
	Pretend to be the teacher.
	Change grades.
	Change attendance.
	Make official promotion decisions.
	Modify school records.
	Produce official examination results.
	Give the student unauthorized access to another student's information.
The AI should help the student learn, not bypass learning.
4.5.24 Student Academic Integrity
Because EduBridge includes assignments, quizzes, assessments and AI, the platform should support academic integrity.
The student shall:
	See assessment rules.
	See whether AI assistance is allowed for an activity.
	See submission requirements.
	Receive warnings about prohibited assistance where configured.
	Confirm submission where required.
Teachers/schools may configure whether an activity allows:
	AI assistance.
	External resources.
	Collaboration.
	Open-book work.
This is important because otherwise the AI assistant could unintentionally undermine the assessment system.

4.5.27 Student Privacy & Access Boundary
The student shall only access information belonging to:
themselves
and information intentionally shared with them.
The student shall not:
	View another student's academic records.
	View another student's attendance.
	View another student's parent information.
	Modify official marks.
	Modify attendance.
	Change their official grade.
	Change their school assignment.
	Change their teacher.
	Modify curriculum standards.
	Publish official examination results.
	Access school administration functions.
	Access teacher-only information.
	Access confidential intervention notes unless explicitly authorized.
	Access government-level analytics.
