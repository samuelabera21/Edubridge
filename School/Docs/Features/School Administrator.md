STEP 1 — SCHOOL PRINCIPAL / SCHOOL ADMINISTRATOR
Role definition
The Principal is the highest operational EduBridge role inside an individual school.
"What is happening in my school, and what action does my school need to take?"
The principal should therefore have school-wide visibility and management authority, while teachers operate classroom-level processes and students/parents operate within their own scope.
1. School Dashboard
The Principal shall have a school-wide dashboard.
It shall display:
	Total students
	Total teachers
	Total school staff
	Number of active classes
	Number of sections
	Number of subjects
	Current academic year
	Enrollment overview
	Student attendance overview
	Teacher attendance overview
	Assessment overview
	Academic performance overview
	Assignment/activity overview
	Students requiring academic support
	Students with attendance problems
	Students at risk of dropping out
	Students with repeated academic difficulties
	Curriculum/teaching progress overview
	School improvement status
	School targets
	Open school issues
	Pending administrative actions
	Important school announcements
	Important education-authority announcements
The dashboard should not simply show numbers.
Each important indicator should allow the principal to drill down:
School
→ Grade
→ Section
→ Subject
→ Teacher / Student
where the principal has authorization to see that information.

The Principal shall be able to view and, where authorized, maintain the school's operational profile.
It shall include:
	School name
	Official school identifier
	Region
	Zone
	Woreda
	Kebele/locality
	School sector/level
	School type
	Ownership
	Urban/rural classification
	School contact information
	Principal information
	Vice-principal information
	Academic year
	Grades offered
	Sections/classes
	School facilities
	School capacity
	School infrastructure information
	School operational status
Important boundary: the Principal should not be able to arbitrarily change an official school identifier or geographic hierarchy. Such information should be controlled by the appropriate higher-level authority/platform process.
3. Academic Year Management
The Principal shall be able to operate the school within an academic year.
They shall be able to:
	View current academic year
	Open/activate the school's operational academic year
	View previous academic years
	Switch between permitted academic years
	View historical school information
	Prepare classes for a new academic year
	Review student promotion information
	Review student repetition information
	Review student transfers
	Review teacher assignments for the new year
	Review subjects offered
	Review sections/classes
The Principal should not define national academic policy.
The platform simply allows the school to operate according to the applicable academic structure.

	View student support history

	View student promotion status

	Assign students to classes/sections

	Review student transfer requests
	Review student status changes
	Identify students without complete records

	Identify inconsistent student information

For example:
Teacher records assessment.
→ Principal can review it.

26. AI Assistant for the Principal
Yes — AI belongs here, but as an assistant, not as an autonomous decision-maker.
The Principal's AI assistant should be able to:
Ask questions
"Which grade has the lowest attendance?"
"Which subjects are performing below the school average?"
"Which students have both low attendance and declining performance?"
"Which classes are behind curriculum progress?"
"What changed compared with last term?"
Summarize
The AI can summarize:
	School performance
	Attendance
	Assessment
	Student support
	Teacher activity
	School improvement
Detect patterns
AI may identify:
	Unusual attendance decline
	Sudden performance decline
	Possible data anomalies
	Repeated student support needs
	Classes requiring attention
	Curriculum delays
Recommend actions
For example:
"Grade 10B has declining mathematics performance and attendance. Consider reviewing teacher feedback, attendance patterns and remedial participation."
But AI should not automatically:
	Fail students
	Promote students
	Change marks
	Change attendance
	Discipline teachers
	Declare a student dropout
	Make official education decisions
Those remain human decisions.

31. What the Principal SHALL NOT do
This boundary is just as important as the features.
The Principal shall not:
	Change national curriculum
	Change national examination results
	Create national education policy
	Modify regional statistics
	Modify Woreda statistics directly
	Access another school's private records
	Access another school's teachers/students
	Change official government identifiers without authorization
	Arbitrarily change historical assessment records
	Arbitrarily change teacher attendance
	Arbitrarily change student attendance
	Automatically determine student dropout through AI
	Automatically determine student failure through AI
	Automatically discipline teachers through AI
The Principal's authority is:
One school, its people, its educational operation, its improvement, and its evidence.
