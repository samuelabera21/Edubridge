STEP 2 — VICE PRINCIPAL / ACADEMIC LEADER
Role definition
The Vice Principal / Academic Leader is the school's day-to-day academic and instructional management role.
Their central question is:
"What is happening in teaching and learning today, and what needs academic attention?"

It shall display:

	Total active classes
	Total sections

	Total students
	Today's scheduled lessons
	Lessons completed

	Student attendance today
	Teacher attendance today
	Pending attendance
	Pending assessments
	Assessment completion
	Average performance

	Academic announcements

24. AI Academic Assistant
This is one of the strongest AI opportunities in EduBridge.
The Vice Principal should be able to ask:
"Which grades are falling behind curriculum progress?"
"Which subjects have the largest performance decline?"
"Which teachers have classes with unusually low attendance?"
"Which students have both declining marks and repeated absence?"
"What changed in Grade 10 mathematics this term?"
"Which intervention programs are actually improving performance?"
"Which classes have the highest assessment completion?"
The AI should return:
Answer
→ evidence
→ affected classes/students
→ trend
→ possible explanation
→ suggested action
The AI should not hide the evidence behind the recommendation.
For example:
"Grade 10B mathematics requires attention."
Then:
	Average decreased 71% → 58%
	Attendance decreased 93% → 81%
	14 students have declining performance
	8 students have repeated absence
	Two assessments were below the previous-term average
Then:
"Consider reviewing attendance, recent assessment difficulty and the current remedial program."
That is a useful decision-support AI.

32. What the Vice Principal SHALL NOT do
This is important because we don't want role overlap.
The Vice Principal shall not:
	Change national curriculum
	Create national examination rules
	Modify national examination results
	Change regional/Woreda statistics
	Manage another school's data
	Change a student's official identity arbitrarily
	Change teacher employment status unless separately authorized
	Arbitrarily change teacher-entered marks
	Arbitrarily change attendance records
	Automatically fail/promote students
	Automatically punish teachers
	Make AI-based disciplinary decisions
	Override the Principal's overall school authority
33. Principal vs Vice Principal — final boundary
This is the distinction I recommend we freeze.
Area	Principal	Vice Principal / Academic Leader
Overall school management	Primary	Support
School identity	Primary	View
Teachers	Overall management	Academic supervision
Students	Overall management	Academic monitoring
Classes/sections	Overall authority	Academic operation
Timetable	Oversight	Primary academic operation
