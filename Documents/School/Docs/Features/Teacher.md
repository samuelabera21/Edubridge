## 4.4 Teacher
The teacher is the primary classroom-level user of EduBridge.
Their responsibility can be summarized as:
Teach → manage learning → record evidence → assess → identify problems → support students → communicate → improve teaching
The teacher should not manage the entire school. Their scope is primarily:
My profile → My assignments → My classes → My students → My teaching → My assessments → My student support
### 4.4.1 Teacher Dashboard
The teacher shall be able to:
- View a personal teaching dashboard.
- View assigned school.
- View academic year.
- View assigned grades.
- View assigned sections/classes.
- View assigned subjects.
- View number of students under their teaching responsibility.
- View today's classes.
- View upcoming classes.
- View pending attendance records.
- View pending assessments.
- View pending assignments.
- View students requiring attention.
- View recent student performance.
- View recent attendance problems.
- View upcoming school activities relevant to the teacher.
- View school announcements.
- View teacher-specific notifications.
- View professional-development notifications.
- View important tasks requiring action.
### AI assistance
The teacher dashboard may provide:
- AI-generated daily teaching summary.
- AI-generated list of students requiring attention.
- AI-generated explanation of unusual attendance/performance patterns.
- AI-generated reminder of pending teacher tasks.
- AI-generated suggested priorities for the day.
AI should assist the teacher, not make official decisions for the teacher.
### 4.4.2 Teacher Profile
The teacher shall be able to:
- View their personal profile.
- View teacher ID.
- View school.
- View academic/professional information authorized by the school.
- View qualifications.
- View subject specialization.
- View teaching experience.
- View subjects they are qualified/assigned to teach.
- View grades they teach.
- View professional-development history.
- View training participation.
- View certificates where applicable.
- Update permitted contact/profile information.
- Request correction of official teacher information.
The platform should distinguish between:
Teacher-editable information
and
School/authorized official information.
For example, a teacher should not simply change their official qualification or employment status themselves.
### 4.4.3 Teaching Assignments
The teacher shall be able to:
- View assigned subjects.
- View assigned grades.
- View assigned sections.
- View assigned classes.
- View number of students per class.
- View teaching load.
- View assigned academic periods.
- View the teacher's relationship with each class/section.
- View changes to teaching assignments.
- Receive notification when an assignment changes.
The teacher should not normally create their own official teaching assignment.
The school assigns:
Teacher → Subject → Grade → Section
EduBridge then makes that assignment available to the teacher.
### 4.4.4 Timetable
The teacher shall be able to:
- View personal timetable.
- View today's timetable.
- View weekly timetable.
- View subject for each period.
- View grade/section for each period.
- View classroom where applicable.
- View lesson duration.
- View timetable changes.
- Receive notifications about timetable changes.
- View cancelled or rescheduled lessons.
The teacher should not arbitrarily modify the official school timetable.
### 4.4.5 Classes and Students
The teacher shall be able to:
- View assigned classes.
- View students belonging to each assigned class.
- Search students.
- View student academic profile within permitted scope.
- View student attendance.
- View student assessment results.
- View student assignment activity.
- View student learning progress.
- View relevant support/intervention information.
- Filter students by performance.
- Filter students by attendance.
- Identify students requiring attention.
- View student trends over time.
The teacher should only see students they are authorized to teach or support.
### 4.4.6 Attendance
This is a core teacher function.
The teacher shall be able to:
- Open a class attendance list.
- Mark students present.
- Mark students absent.
- Mark students late.
- Record an authorized attendance status where applicable.
- View previously recorded attendance.
- Correct an attendance entry within the permitted correction period.
- Submit an attendance correction request where direct correction is no longer allowed.
- Add an appropriate attendance note/reason where permitted.
- View attendance history.
- View attendance percentage.
- Identify students with repeated absence.
- Identify students with repeated lateness.
- Submit attendance for a lesson/class.
- Receive reminders when attendance has not been submitted.
The system should maintain an audit trail for changes.
### AI assistance
AI may:
- Detect unusual attendance patterns.
- Highlight students with rapidly increasing absence.
- Identify combinations such as low attendance + declining performance.
- Suggest which students may require follow-up.
- Generate a concise attendance summary for the teacher.
AI should not automatically change attendance records.
### 4.4.7 Lesson / Teaching Record
The teacher shall be able to record the important information surrounding teaching.
For example:
Class → Subject → Lesson → Topic → Date → Status
The teacher shall be able to:
- Record that a lesson was conducted.
- Record the topic covered.
- Select the relevant curriculum unit/topic where configured.
- Record lesson date.
- Record class/section.
- Record number of students attending.
- Record lesson status.
- Record optional teaching notes.
- Record learning difficulties observed.
- Record important classroom observations.
- Record whether the planned lesson was completed.
- Record reasons for significant interruption where appropriate.
### 4.4.8 Curriculum / Lesson Progress
The teacher shall be able to:
- View the curriculum/subject structure applicable to their class.
- View expected topics/units.
- Mark topics/units as covered.
- Record teaching progress.
- View completed topics.
- View pending topics.
- Compare planned progress with actual progress.
- Record reasons for significant delays where appropriate.
- Identify classes falling behind.
- Report curriculum implementation difficulties.
### AI assistance
AI may:
- Compare expected and actual progress.
- Detect classes falling behind.
- Summarize curriculum progress.
- Suggest planning adjustments.
- Suggest revision activities when appropriate.
- Help the teacher prepare a catch-up plan.
AI should not independently change the official curriculum.
### 4.4.9 Assessment
This is another core teacher function.
The teacher shall be able to:
- Create an assessment.
- Define assessment title.
- Select class/section.
- Select subject.
- Select assessment type.
- Define assessment date.
- Define maximum score.
- Enter student scores.
- Edit scores before final submission.
- Submit/finalize assessment results.
- View assessment results.
- View class average.
- View student averages.
- View highest/lowest performance.
- View pass/failure distribution according to configured rules.
- View performance by assessment.
- View performance by topic/competency where supported.
- View student performance trends.
- Provide teacher feedback.
- Record appropriate assessment remarks.
- Export/print permitted assessment reports.
Assessment types can include, where applicable:
- Quiz
- Test
- Assignment
- Homework
- Project
- Practical assessment
- Oral assessment
- Class work
- Continuous assessment
- Midterm/internal examination
The exact assessment types and grading rules should be configurable according to the applicable school/education level rather than hard-coded universally.
### 4.4.10 Student Performance Monitoring
The teacher shall be able to view a consolidated picture of each assigned student.
For each student, the teacher may see:
- Academic performance.
- Subject performance.
- Assessment history.
- Attendance.
- Assignment completion.
- Learning activity.
- Performance trend.
- Teacher feedback.
- Support/intervention history relevant to the teacher.
The teacher shall be able to:
- Identify low-performing students.
- Identify high-performing students.
- Identify declining performance.
- Identify improving performance.
- Identify students with repeated assessment difficulties.
- Compare class performance.
- Identify common learning difficulties.
- Monitor improvement after intervention.



### 4.4.11 Student Support & Intervention
The teacher shall be able to:
- Identify a student requiring academic support.
- Record the identified learning difficulty.
- Create a support/intervention recommendation.
- Recommend remedial work.
- Recommend enrichment activities.
- Assign a support activity.
- Record intervention date.
- Record intervention type.
- Record intervention notes.
- Track intervention status.
- Record follow-up results.
- Compare performance before and after intervention.
- Refer a student to appropriate school support.
- Notify relevant school staff where required.
- Communicate appropriate support information to parents.
### AI assistance
AI may:
- Identify students who may benefit from intervention.
- Detect patterns across attendance and assessment.
- Suggest possible areas of difficulty.
- Suggest remedial activities.
- Suggest enrichment activities for high-performing students.
- Generate intervention summaries.
- Compare pre/post intervention performance.
But AI should say:
"Student may require additional support."
not:
"Student will fail."
The final educational judgment remains with the teacher/school.

### 4.4.12 Assignments & Learning Activities
The teacher shall be able to:
- Create an assignment.
- Select class/section.
- Select subject.
- Add instructions.
- Set due date.
- Attach learning material where permitted.
- Assign the activity to students.
- View assigned activities.
- Track submission status.
- View submitted work.
- Record scores.
- Provide feedback.
- Mark completion.
- Return/reopen an activity where permitted.
- View assignment completion statistics.
- Identify students who have not completed activities.
### AI assistance
The teacher may use AI to:
- Draft assignment instructions.
- Generate practice questions.
- Generate differentiated practice.
- Generate examples.
- Suggest assessment questions.
- Create revision activities.
- Summarize student submissions where appropriate.
- Suggest feedback.
However, AI-generated content should remain teacher-reviewed before being presented as official teaching/assessment material.



### 4.4.13 Learning Resources
The teacher shall be able to:
- Search available approved learning resources.
- View resources relevant to their subject.
- View resources by grade.
- View resources by topic.
- Share resources with assigned students.
- Attach resources to assignments.
- Recommend resources for remedial support.
- Recommend enrichment resources.

### 4.4.14 Teacher–Student Communication
The teacher shall be able to:
- Send messages to assigned students.
- Send class announcements.
- Send section announcements.
- Respond to student questions.
- Send academic feedback.
- Send reminders.
- Communicate support recommendations.
- Receive relevant student messages.
- Restrict communication to authorized educational contexts.
The system should preserve communication history where appropriate.
### 4.4.15 Parent Communication
The teacher shall be able to:
- Contact a student's parent/guardian through authorized channels.
- Send attendance-related notifications.
- Send academic-progress notifications.
- Send assignment reminders.
- Send support recommendations.
- Provide teacher feedback.
- Request a parent meeting.
- Respond to parent messages.
- View relevant parent communication history.
The teacher should not expose another student's information to a parent.
### 4.4.16 Teacher Announcements
The teacher shall be able to:
- Create a class announcement.
- Send an announcement to a section.
- Send an announcement to assigned students.
- Schedule an announcement.
- Attach relevant materials.
- View announcement delivery/read status where supported.
School-wide announcements remain under the appropriate school authority.
### 4.4.17 Teacher Professional Development
The teacher shall be able to:
- View assigned professional-development activities.
- View available training.
- Register/participate where the workflow allows.
- Track training status.
- View completed training.
- View certificates where available.
- Upload permitted professional-development evidence.
- View professional-development history.
- Receive training reminders.
- Receive relevant professional-development recommendations.
### AI assistance
AI may:
- Identify possible professional-development areas from teaching activity.
- Recommend relevant resources/training.
- Summarize completed professional-development activities.
It should not make official employment or promotion decisions.


### 4.4.18 Classroom / School Problem Reporting
The teacher shall be able to report issues affecting teaching and learning, such as:
- Lack of textbooks.
- Lack of teaching materials.
- Classroom overcrowding.
- Laboratory/resource problems.
- ICT/internet problems.
- Classroom facility problems.
- Curriculum implementation problems.
- Student welfare concerns.
- Safety concerns.
- Accessibility problems.
- Other teaching/learning obstacles.
The report should flow to the appropriate school authority.
Example:
Teacher → School Administration → Action → Resolution
The teacher should be able to track the status of submitted issues where the school enables this.
### 4.4.19 Teacher Reports
The teacher shall be able to generate/view permitted reports such as:
- Class attendance report.
- Student attendance report.
- Assessment report.
- Student performance report.
- Subject performance report.
- Assignment completion report.
- Curriculum progress report.
- Student support/intervention report.
- Class activity report.
- Parent communication history where appropriate.
The teacher's reporting scope remains:
My teaching and my assigned students.
The teacher should not have access to regional, woreda, or other-school confidential analytics.
### 4.4.20 Teacher Notifications
The teacher shall receive notifications for:
Missing attendance submission.
Upcoming assessment.
Assessment requiring completion.
Assignment deadline.
Student support alert.
Repeated student absence.
Significant performance decline.
Parent message.
Student message.
School announcement.
Timetable change.
Professional-development activity.
School task requiring teacher action.
### 4.4.21 Teacher AI Assistant
I would make this a dedicated capability, rather than scattering AI everywhere.
The Teacher AI Assistant shall be able to help with:
Teaching preparation
- Generate lesson-plan drafts.
- Suggest lesson activities.
- Suggest examples.
- Suggest classroom questions.
- Suggest revision activities.
- Adapt explanations to different student levels.
Assessment
- Generate draft quiz questions.
- Generate practice questions.
- Generate question variations.
- Suggest assessment structure.
- Analyze class assessment results.
- Identify commonly missed areas.
Student support
- Identify students who may need attention.
- Explain performance patterns.
- Suggest remedial activities.
- Suggest enrichment activities.
- Generate intervention summaries.
Administrative assistance
- Summarize attendance.
- Summarize class performance.
- Draft parent messages.
- Draft class announcements.
- Draft reports.
- Summarize teacher activity.
Resource assistance
- Find relevant approved resources.
- Recommend learning resources.
- Explain curriculum topics.
- Help generate supplementary learning material.
Important AI boundary:
The AI assistant shall recommend, draft, summarize, and analyze.
It shall not independently:
- Change grades.
- Change attendance.
- Promote students.
- Fail students.
- Change official records.
- Make disciplinary decisions.
- Make employment decisions.
- Publish official examination results.
### 4.4.22 Teacher Data Correction
The teacher shall be able to:
- Request correction of their own profile information.
- Request correction of student records within permitted scope.
- Request correction of attendance.
- Request correction of assessment information.
- Provide a reason for a correction.
- View correction-request status.
- See whether the correction was approved/rejected.
For finalized/locked records, the teacher should not silently overwrite historical information.
This gives EduBridge a traceable educational record rather than allowing unrestricted editing.
### 4.4.23 Teacher Privacy & Access Boundary
The teacher shall only access information necessary for their role.
The teacher shall:
- See their assigned classes.
- See their assigned students.
- See relevant student educational information.
- See their own teaching information.
- See appropriate school announcements.
- See their own reports.
The teacher shall not:
- Manage the entire school.
- Create or delete the school.
- Change official school identity.
- Change another teacher's assignments.
- Change another teacher's assessment records.
- View another school's students.
- View regional confidential information.
- Modify Ministry statistics.
- Change national examination results.
- Change official curriculum standards.
- Approve student promotion independently.
- Manage school-wide permissions.

The core principle
The teacher module should not become another generic HR portal or LMS.
The teacher is the person closest to the actual learning process.
So EduBridge should help the teacher do five things exceptionally well:
1. Teach
2. Record
3. Assess
4. Identify and support students
5. Communicate
Everything else should support those five responsibilities.
